# Rubric — check your own render

Run this on the finished piece, looking at the actual render (or the slide in your tool), not at
your source. Answer each question yes or no. Any "no" is fixed before you hand the piece over;
do not explain it away. When you hand it over, list the placeholders from R10.

## R1
Can someone say what the canvas is about, and who it is from, within three seconds?

## R2
Is the headline at least three times the body text size, and is it visibly the first thing read?

## R3
Do headline, body and image edges sit on the format's grid and shared margins, with asymmetric
room (more at the top and reading side than at the bottom)?

## R4
Is at least a third of the canvas empty, and are all elements outside the format's `safeArea` margin?

## R5
Is every text pair readable: at least 4.5:1 for body, 3:1 for large text, and no white text on an
accent hue?

## R6
Is there exactly one cube motif (or one pattern field), used large and cropped, and not behind text?

## R7
Is there one band or accent hue per section, with ink text or the paired tint-ink?

## R8
Is the density within the format's limit: words per canvas at or below `maxWords`, headline at or
below `maxHeadlineChars`, no text below `minText`?

## R9
Is it all flat: no gradients, textures, glows, or shadows beyond the system's?

## R10
Is every figure the user's own or a visible placeholder, every image a labelled placeholder naming
the shot, and are the placeholders listed to the user?

## R11
Is all reader-facing copy pt-BR, in sentence case, with no emoji, and with Montserrat (or a flagged
stand-in) as the only typeface?

## R12
Is the selo official artwork, unmodified, clear of other elements by X on all sides, and above its
minimum size (45 mm in print, 150 px on screen), without the Sesc signature next to it?
