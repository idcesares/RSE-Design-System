Accessibility

Contrast

Body text (--rse-color-text-body on --rse-color-surface-card/--rse-color-surface-page) meets WCAG AA. Never place --rse-color-text-muted on colored accent backgrounds — pair muted text with white/cream surfaces only. On brand-purple or ink fills, always use --rse-color-text-on-primary/white, never gray.

Focus

Every interactive element needs a visible focus state using --rse-color-focus-ring (a 3px tinted ring, as seen on Input/Checkbox/Radio focus). Don't rely on color change alone — pair with the ring, not just a border-color swap.

Motion

All transitions are short (150–360ms) and non-essential to comprehension. Wrap any future scroll-triggered or looping motion in @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) to disable it — the brand's calm, unhurried tone should degrade gracefully, not jitter.

Keyboard

Tabs/Pagination buttons are native <button> elements — Tab/Enter/Space work by default. Modal should trap focus and close on Escape when wired up by the consumer (the primitive provides the backdrop-click/close-button hooks; focus-trap is the integrator's responsibility). Tooltip content should also be reachable on keyboard focus, not just hover.

Alt text & labels

Every <img> and icon-only IconButton needs real alt/aria-label text in Portuguese describing the content or action — never decorative filler like "imagem" or the filename.
