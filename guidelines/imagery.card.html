Direction

✓Documentary, in-situ: real classrooms, real students, natural light.
✓Warm tones that sit next to the cream page; skin tones true and unfiltered.
✓The student is the protagonist — action and attention, not posed rows.
✓Visible plurality: ages, regions, abilities, body types, skin tones.
✓Room in the frame for a headline when the image is full-bleed.
✓Enquadramento aberto ou de cima — a cena inteira, uniforme Sesc visível.
✓Direção confirmada pelas seis pranchas fotográficas do manual (docs/brand-manual.md).
✕Stock-looking staged shots, thumbs-up, empty smiling-at-camera groups.
✕Heavy filters, duotones, purple washes, gradient overlays on photos.
✕Text baked into the image; captions and headlines stay in HTML.
✕AI-generated or illustrated people anywhere in the system.
✕Photos as section backgrounds behind body copy.

Legibility over photography

full-bleed photo

Aprender para a vida acontecer

When text must sit on a photo, use a single-direction ink scrim (--color-ink at 70%→0%), never a purple tint and never a blur. Minimum 4.5:1 on the darkest part of the text.

Every photo needs alt text describing what is happening, not "foto de aluno". Credit and consent (including image release for minors) are a requirement, not a nicety.
