Duration & easing

fast · 150ms

Press, focus, colour swaps.

base · 220ms

Hover, open/close, accordions.

slow · 360ms

Modals, page-level reveals. Nothing slower.

Easing is --ease-standard for state changes and --ease-out for anything entering. No spring, no bounce, no overshoot — the brand reads institutional, not playful.

Rules

  • Animate transform, opacity, background and box-shadow only — never layout properties.
  • No scroll-triggered motion, parallax, or autoplaying decorative animation.
  • Motion carries meaning (state change, progress, arrival) or it is removed.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: reduce is honoured globally in tokens/base.css; never re-introduce motion inline in a way that escapes it.
